## Promotion Policy

### 20% Promotion

Promotion code SAVE20 provides 20% off eligible products.

Eligibility:
- The promotion must be valid and active.
- The product must be eligible for the promotion.
- The customer must enter the promotion code correctly.
- The promotion may not be combined with certain other promotions.

The promotion may not apply to products that are explicitly
excluded from the promotion.

Do not assume that every product is eligible for the promotion.

## Return Policy

Running shoes can normally be returned within 30 calendar days
after delivery if they are unused and in resalable condition.

Shoes that have been worn outdoors may be rejected if they show
signs of use.

Non-returnable items:
- Personalized products.
- Final-sale products.
- Products explicitly marked as non-returnable.
- Certain hygiene-sensitive products.

Refund:
- After the returned item passes inspection, the refund is normally
  issued to the original payment method.
- Refund processing may take several business days after the return
  is approved.

Example:
If an item was delivered on August 1, a standard return request
should be submitted by August 31, provided the item meets the
eligibility conditions.

---

## Delivery Policy

Standard delivery normally takes 3-5 business days.

Delivery times may vary depending on the destination and fulfillment
location.

---

## Shipping Policy

Shipping availability and delivery options depend on the product,
destination, and fulfillment location.

Multiple items in an order may be shipped separately when they are
fulfilled from different locations.

## Order Split Policy

An order may be split into multiple shipments when items are
fulfilled from different locations.

Example:

An order contains one pair of shoes available at Location A and
one accessory available at Location B.

The commerce system may create two shipments so that each item can
be fulfilled from the appropriate location.
